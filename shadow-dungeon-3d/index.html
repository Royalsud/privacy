Privacy Policy

Shadow Dungeon 3D

A single-player, offline 3D dungeon crawler for Android.

Package: com.sudip.shadowdungeon3d
Developer: Sudip Mondal
Effective date: 15 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 15 September 2026

Shadow Dungeon 3D does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The game runs entirely on your device, with no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access at all.

  • No personal information is requested or collected
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting
  • No accounts, sign-in or online features
  • No internet permission — the app cannot send data anywhere
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind

1.Information we collect

None. Shadow Dungeon 3D does not ask for, collect, or transmit any personal or sensitive information. Specifically, the game does not access or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, calendar, location, photos, files, camera, microphone, or any advertising or device identifier.

2.Information stored on your device

So that you can carry on where you left off, the game saves your game data in its own private storage area on your device. This data never leaves your device. It consists of:

  • Game progress — current run, floor and zone, rooms cleared, best depth reached, coins, experience and unlocks.
  • Settings — graphics quality, camera and screen-shake options, music and sound volume, vibration on/off and strength, control sensitivity.
  • Cosmetic choices — the theme, hero and weapon you have selected.
  • An optional display name (nickname) of up to 14 characters that you may type on the Customise screen. It defaults to “HERO”. It is shown only on your own screen, inside the game. It is not verified, not linked to any account, and is never transmitted.

This save file lives in the app's private folder, is excluded from Google's automatic app backup, and is permanently deleted when you uninstall the game or clear the app's storage from Android Settings.

3.Permissions

Shadow Dungeon 3D requests no Android permissions whatsoever — including no internet permission. The game uses your phone's vibration motor for combat feedback, which requires no permission and can be turned off or weakened at any time in the game's audio and haptics settings.

4.Third parties

The game contains no advertising networks, no analytics or attribution services, no crash reporting, no social network integrations and no third-party software development kits. Because no data is collected, no data is shared, sold, rented or disclosed to anyone.

5.Children's privacy

The game collects no data from any user, including children. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no chat and no links out to other services. As nothing is collected, there is nothing that could be collected from a child under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

6.Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal data a developer holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to look up, export or erase. The game data stored on your own device is entirely under your control: uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, removes all of it immediately and permanently.

7.Security

Because no data is transmitted or stored on any server, there is no account to be breached and no data of yours in our possession. Your saved game is protected by Android's standard app-sandbox isolation, which prevents other apps from reading it.

8.Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds a feature that does handle data — for example online leaderboards or advertising — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change. The current policy always applies to the version of the game available on Google Play.
